RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Code of conduct - RIGHT ARM

01

Confidentiality

All information within RIGHT ARM is strictly confidential. Sharing internal data, project details, or member identities with outsiders will result in immediate termination and permanent ban.

02

Ethical Operations Only

All activities must remain within legal boundaries. RIGHT ARM operates exclusively for ethical hacking, security research, and defensive purposes. Any illegal activity is strictly prohibited.

03

Respect the Chain of Command

Follow orders from higher-ranking operatives. The command structure exists for operational efficiency and security.

04

Credential Security

Never share your login credentials with anyone. You are responsible for all actions taken under your account. Report any suspicious activity immediately.

05

No Internal Conflict

Attacking or undermining fellow members is forbidden. We operate as one unit. Internal disputes must be reported to Command for resolution.

06

Activity Requirement

Members must remain active. Prolonged inactivity without notice will result in removal from operations. Notify Command if you need leave.

07

Represent RIGHT ARM

Your actions reflect on the entire community. Maintain professionalism in all external interactions. Do not damage our reputation.

08

Zero Tolerance

Violation of these rules will result in disciplinary action ranging from warning to permanent expulsion. Decisions by the Commander are final.
